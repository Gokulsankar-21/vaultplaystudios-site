Privacy Policy for Dragon Doku: The Puzzle Game

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains what information Dragon Doku: The Puzzle Game ("the game") uses and why. The game is made by Vault Play Studios, run by Gokulsankar Thangaraj in Tamil Nadu, India ("we", "us"). If you have any question, write to hello@vaultplaystudios.com.

The short version

  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location, and we have no servers of our own.
  • Your game progress is saved on your phone. If you sign in with Google Play Games, it can also be saved in your Google account.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob and sells optional items through Google Play. Google handles these, under Google's own privacy policy.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (levels, stars, scores, tools, streaks, settings) in a file on your phone. This file stays on your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or use Settings → Reset all progress.

Google Play Games Services

You can sign in with Google Play Games to back up your progress, use leaderboards and achievements, and continue on another phone. When you sign in, Google shares your Play Games player ID and profile name with the game, and your saved progress and scores are stored by Google. You can manage or delete this data in the Google Play Games app and in your Google account. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, basic device information (model, operating system, language), your IP address (which gives an approximate location), and how you interact with ads.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice any time with Settings → Privacy options.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your phone).
  • We ask AdMob to show only ads suitable for a general audience.

Learn how Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional purchases, such as removing ads or tool packs, are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or bank details. Google tells the game only what you bought, so it can deliver the item and restore it later.

Crash and performance data

Google Play may collect anonymous crash reports and performance data from your phone (Android vitals) if you allow this in your phone's settings. We use these reports only to fix problems.

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has given us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

How long information is kept

Progress on your phone is kept until you uninstall the game or reset it. Data held by Google (Play Games, AdMob, Google Play) is kept according to Google's policies. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we have about you, to correct it or to have it deleted. Because we do not keep personal information on our own servers, most requests are handled through your Google account settings, but you can always write to us and we will help.

Security

We use Google's services, which protect data in transit and at rest. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure, but we keep what we use to a minimum.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. Big changes will also be mentioned in the game's update notes.
